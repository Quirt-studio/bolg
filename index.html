About

The Person Behind the Work

Technology is a craft. Life is the canvas.

I'm a multi-disciplinary creator based in China, working at the intersection of software development, visual arts, and everyday aesthetics. I believe that the same care one puts into writing elegant code should extend to how one makes a cocktail, practices calligraphy, or frames a shot.

This site is my digital studio — a space where engineering meets art, where structured thinking meets spontaneous creativity, and where the line between "work" and "life" is intentionally blurred.

Currently exploring: AI-assisted creative workflows, generative art systems, and the philosophy of slow technology.
